# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect personal, customer, and confidential information.
- Respect project scope, maintainer decisions, and contributor time.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, publishing private information, sustained disruption, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate is not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact method listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), clearly marking the report as a conduct issue. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
